# Essam's level and taste (read before writing strips)

Update this file after reading new answers in the page's `answers` database.

## Level (as of 2026-10-11, 8 strips, all graded "got")
- Everyday vocabulary is fine. New words he reports (今更, バラバラ) were understood first try.
- Plain grammar only: polite/plain forms, ～ている, ～たい, ～から, ～けど, ～のに, conditionals.
- Avoid casual contractions and particles he does not know yet: ～ちゃう / ～じゃう, sentence-final もん,
  dropped い (写ってない → 写っていない). 傘 #2 failed until he translated 「ぬれちゃうもん」.
- Keep the punchline line the plainest line in the strip. Speakers must be obvious
  (he once attributed the son's line to the cat in 鍵 #2).
- Grandpa's role speech (わし, ～じゃ, ～のう) is fine in small doses.
- 鍵 #1 took 2–3 listens: long narration lines are harder by ear; keep narration short.

## Taste
- Best: a twist with a callback or escalation (傘 #1, “六本目だよ” made him laugh; 今更 curry; バラバラ family day).
- Weaker: a mild, predictable jab (鏡 #2 birthday/mirror: “a little less fun”).
- Prefer punchlines where the last line flips or escalates the situation, not just comments on it.
